The homepage keeps four compact columns in the original vertical-title design: configurator, chatbot, calculator, advisor. Smaller screens use two columns or one column with internal widget scrolling.

The two MôjPlot cards use static high-resolution captures of its original widget and calculator, including their own logo and product photographs. The homepage previews are read-only. The pergola uses a 12-second, 60 fps, 800 × 600 H.264 film rendered from the original model, with louver and ZIP motion, three frame colours and an underside LED detail. It plays only while visible and pauses for hidden tabs and reduced motion. The interactive model remains on its dedicated page. Portfolio and preview images use contain sizing, so they show the full captured interface without cropping. Four staggered cards retain the original vertical headings and top-corner arrows.

The carousel contains six screenshots of progressive choices in Koverta's original Soltec renderer. It contains no live configurator. All frames use the same 5,076 × 2,500mm footprint: an anthracite base, white frame, contrasting anthracite louvers, ZIP screen, an underside LED detail and the final combination. Captures originate at 2800 × 1750, are exported as 1400 × 875 WebP, and have separate 400 × 250 thumbnails. The six images and descriptions now progress with page scroll inside a sticky scene. There are no carousel click controls or autoplay timers. The opening step describes choosing a shelter to suit the customer’s needs; this example then continues with a bioclimatic pergola.

The live pergola demo uses the original geometry and WebGL renderer. Its repeated 22-second tour updates the model on browser animation frames while throttling React label updates. It demonstrates louvers, ZIP, full assembly color and LED lighting. Manual interaction stops the tour until Play is selected. Camera motion uses the renderer's frame scheduler, avoiding duplicate drawing; unchanged updates do not rebuild the model. Embedded previews avoid temporal subpixel jitter, and card hover no longer moves the whole renderer. LED materials emit light and participate in the renderer's bloom, instead of being shaded like roof panels. LED selection turns the camera towards the underside; furniture is hidden for this roof detail. The original seating models, missing in the prior handoff, are now included. Their source and license notes are in `public/work/pergola/scene-assets/CREDITS.md`.
